ORIEL-1 / ENGINEERING RECORD FORMAT / 1.3

Response type 90 followed by ten 32-bit little-endian words (40 bytes):
 00 signature       314b484f (ASCII OHK1)
 04 uptime_ms       Onboard milliseconds since OBC reset
 08 bus_mv          Supply millivolts
 0c temperature     Centidegrees Celsius
 10 reset_count     Supervisor reset counter
 14 samples         Completed instrument samples since OBC reset
 18 mode            1 = scheduled engineering acquisition
 1c firmware        0103 = archived release 1.3
 20 photodiode      Unsigned instrument sample
 24 integrity       Wrapping unsigned sum of the first nine words

The record checksum is separate from the link-frame CRC. A valid radio
frame can contain an invalid engineering record. The decoder checks both
record length and signature/integrity. Sensor ranges, timestamps, freshness,
and progression still require operator judgment; 'valid' is not a complete
statement about spacecraft health.

The timer produces a record every 30 seconds. The engineering service
requests an additional record. Some packets may be lost or sent below the
horizon. The sample count is reset by an OBC restart. The link beacon clock
is seconds, whereas engineering record uptime is milliseconds.
