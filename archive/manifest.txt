The bench loads flight.bin, with nominal memory and devices. The source
exports are not a complete build tree. In particular, maintenance-v12.asm
is an earlier revision and should not be assumed byte-identical to 1.3.
Binaries, symbol maps and source exports can be compared with local tools.
Historical logs were hand-transcribed from accepted records; they do not
represent a complete lossless recording of all radio traffic.
